I'm Anirudh Rajagopalan, a systems engineer on NVIDIA's Software-Defined Networking team. I build infrastructure where correctness, performance, security, and operability all matter at once.

I am most interested in the layers beneath an application: how packets move, how distributed components coordinate, how policies are enforced, how failures propagate, and how a system remains understandable as it scales. My work spans cloud networking, Kubernetes, distributed services, control planes, and low-level performance.

Today, I work on SDN at NVIDIA. Previously, I worked on networking and security for NVIDIA DGX Cloud and on large-scale service infrastructure at Oracle Cloud. I earned an M.S. in Computer Science from USC and a B.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering from NIT Tiruchirappalli.

What I work on
------

* **Cloud networking:** software-defined networks, OVN-Kubernetes, OpenFlow, network policy, and packet filtering.
* **Distributed infrastructure:** service orchestration, fault tolerance, asynchronous processing, and resource management.
* **Systems security:** policy enforcement, isolation boundaries, and privacy-preserving protocols.
* **Performance:** profiling bottlenecks, parallel execution, and designing for predictable behavior under load.

Selected work
------

* [Scaler](/portfolio/scaler/) — a fault-tolerant distributed computing framework built with Go, Kubernetes, and gRPC.
* [Zero Barrier](/portfolio/zero-barrier/) — infrastructure automation for deploying multi-service applications on Google Cloud.
* [SeqToPar](/portfolio/seq-to-par/) — CUDA implementations of parallel algorithms and performance comparisons against sequential versions.
* [GenericCoin](/portfolio/generic-coin/) — a blockchain implementation built to explore consensus, transaction validation, and cryptographic integrity.
